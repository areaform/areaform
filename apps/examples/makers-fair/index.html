Getting in

Tickets are €12 a day at the door, free under 16. Two entrances on the south side of the hall; registration is one floor down.

Step-free

A lift reaches every floor. Ask the plan for a step-free route and it avoids the stairs.

Food

The café by the north wall is open all day, with a quiet lounge upstairs.
